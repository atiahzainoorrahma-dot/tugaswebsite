Campus Organization

My University Experience

At Universitas Negeri Medan, I continue to grow by being actively involved in student organizations. I've been part of IMA PGPAUD, also known as HMJ (Department Student Association), for around two years, having had the opportunity to join for two different periods.

Throughout my time in IMA PGPAUD, I've taken part in various activities that have broadened my knowledge, encouraged me to step out of my comfort zone, and helped me improve my public speaking and self-confidence.

“For me, being part of an organization is more than just joining activities. It's also about learning, building connections, and becoming a better version of myself.”

Teaching Practicum

My Teaching Internship

During my third semester, I took part in an independent internship at Prestige Bilingual School Kindergarten in Medan. It was a valuable experience that allowed me to learn more about the world of early childhood education.

I learned how to teach young children, build connections with them, and create learning materials alongside the teachers. I also had the chance to take part in various classroom activities and observe different ways of supporting children's learning and development.

Since I'm comfortable using English in everyday conversations, I occasionally used English to communicate with the children during my internship. This experience helped me improve my teaching skills and gave me a better understanding of what it means to be an early childhood educator.
