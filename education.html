My High School Journey

During high school, I studied Social Sciences and actively participated in two student organizations: the Student Council (OSIS) and the Youth Red Cross (PMR).

In OSIS, I was a member of the Nutrition and Health Division. Being involved in these organizations gave me valuable opportunities to learn beyond the classroom. I joined because I wanted to broaden my knowledge, develop my leadership skills, and become more confident in public speaking.

These experiences helped me grow, work with others, and discover the importance of being an active and responsible student.
