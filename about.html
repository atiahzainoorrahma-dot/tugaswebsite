About Me

A glimpse into my background, my journey, and what drives my passion for early education.

Hi! I'm Atiah Zainoor Rahma

I'm Atiah Zainoor Rahma, a 19-year-old student at Universitas Negeri Medan, majoring in Early Childhood Teacher Education. I'm a graduate of SMA Negeri 3 Medan, where I studied Social Sciences.

I'm someone who enjoys learning new things, trying new experiences, and developing myself along the way. I'm especially interested in early childhood education because I love learning about how children grow, learn, and explore the world around them.
